The line chart shows the percentage of patients meeting their weekly 150-minute exercise goal across four follow-up visits. The x-axis shows the follow-up visit number, and the y-axis shows the percentage of patients meeting the goal. Home-based patients increased from 58% at visit 1 to 70% at visit 4, while center-based patients increased from 57% to 79%. At visit 4, center-based patients had a 9-percentage-point higher rate (79% versus 70%). Patients chose their program, so this does not establish causation. Each percentage also includes only patients seen at that visit, which is approximately 45 per program, so the difference could be due to random variation or differences in the patients who returned.
